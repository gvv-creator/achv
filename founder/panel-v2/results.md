## What would flip a no

- A price at or below 12 euro a m2 all-in, plus a finished Maltese block where the coating peeled off cleanly and damage claims dropped.
- A Maltese block I can visit where it came off clean, written confirmation from the coating maker for my glass and frames, and a price at or under 12 euro a m2 all in.
- A main contractor writing protection into the subcontract package as a paid line, plus written coating-maker compatibility approval for my glass coatings and frame finishes and a Maltese reference job at or below about €12 a m².
- The main contractor or developer specifying it as a separate paid line in the subcontract package, plus a local reference job showing it saved more in damage than it cost.
- A Maltese reference block where a contractor like us saw damage cost clearly above 19 per m2, plus the compatibility letter for our glass and frame finish.
- A main contractor or developer who puts it in the tender as a paid line, so it is not my cost. Or a price at the cost of a roll of film, with a local reference job showing no glass or frame damage.
- Written compatibility confirmation from the coating maker for our specific glass coatings and frame finishes. A Maltese reference on a glazed block. A guarantee that only an independent applicator touches our site, with no ACHV visits beyond a test panel I control.
- Seeing it on a finished local block of similar size, and the window installer I use telling me it works and the glass came out clean.
- A Maltese reference on a similar small block and a price near film and tape, or a glazing sub or developer who asks for it and pays for it.
- A Maltese block finished with it, with documented before-and-after damage figures and the coating maker's written compatibility confirmation for our glass and frame finish, at a price close to what we pay today.
- A written compatibility confirmation from the coating maker for my exact glass coating and frame finish, plus a test panel left in summer sun and removed cleanly, at a price near what I pay now.
- A local reference block where I can see the damage savings, and a price close to 5 to 6 euro per m2 all-in, or proof that my last block lost more to glazing damage than this would cost.

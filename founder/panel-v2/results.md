## What would flip a no

- A higher headline price with all the money paid at closing, or a seller who will vouch for them.
- Meeting the directors in person, my adviser confirming the structure and the bank letter, and a reference from a seller or a long-held business they already run.
- A higher price, 100% cash at closing or the full price in escrow, and a reference from a seller who has already sold to them, or a known, established backer I can check.
- Two or three former sellers or comparable owners I can call who say you kept your word, plus my tax adviser telling me the terms are sound.
- Two or three earlier sellers I can call, and my own adviser confirming the buyer and the funds.
- Meeting the named directors in person, visiting a business they already own, and my own adviser confirming the bank letter and guarantee are real; longer protection for staff than 24 months.
- My own bank and tax adviser confirming the funds and the directors, plus the full cash price paid at closing with a notary-secured contract.
- My own tax adviser and bank vouching for them after checking, and meeting the directors in person at my site and hearing them speak to my staff.
- My own adviser and bank confirming their funds and directors check out, plus a reference from someone who has dealt with them, or longer staff and site guarantees.
- A seller who already sold to them and will talk to me, plus all 100 percent in cash at closing, or the 10 percent paid into my adviser's escrow account at closing.
- A reference from a previous seller or from a bank or adviser I know and trust, plus a bank letter checked by my own adviser for the full price.
- A reference from a seller they have already bought from, or a named owner I can phone, plus the staff and name guarantees written into the contract as enforceable terms.

Protected Handover (ACHV Performance Systems, using HEVADEX OMNIGUARD): a peelable coating applied to installed glazing and aluminium or PVC frames that protects them from cement, plaster, paint and scratches until handover, then peels off. It is quoted as a separate line, so the main contractor, the developer or the glazing subcontractor, whoever carries handover damage, can specify it and pay for it.
Price: €12.26 per m² to ACHV (product, survey, test panel, removal inspection, signed QA pack) plus the applicator's labour, about €6.70 per m², so about €19 per m² ex VAT. Minimum 100 m², one floor. Removal is scheduled in the quote; the coating must come off within 18 months on windows. If you prefer your own crew: OMNIGUARD supplied at €15.50 per kg (about €10.20 per m²) with the method statement.
Before you order: a free test panel on your own frames and glass. ACHV asks the coating maker for written compatibility guidance for your glass coating and frame finish and shows you the replies. We also work out with you what handover damage cost you on your last block, so you can see whether it pays.
If the coating or its removal damages your glass or frames, ACHV pays the documented repair or replacement, up to €2,500 per job, written into the quote (applied and removed as the method statement says, on the listed surfaces).
ACHV staff visit only for the test panel, the first-job check and the removal inspection, by appointment, under a written confidentiality undertaking about your programme. An independent approved applicator does the work.
Payment: 60 days from invoice after a credit check, no deposit.
Founding sites: for the first three sites, ACHV charges €7.28 per m² (about €14 all-in) for a documented pilot of 100 to 150 m². In return you let us record before-and-after damage and, with your approval, show the finished block. There is no Maltese reference job yet; these will be the first.

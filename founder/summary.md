# Summary: Always Broke Ltd

**What it is.** A Belize-incorporated holding company that buys profitable owner-managed small and micro businesses in the EU and keeps them for the long term. It is for owners with no successor (and their advisers and banks), and for the funders who would provide the capital.

**Verdict: Not yet.** The check that fails is the one that rests on evidence: the realistic year-1 revenue the account panel supports is **€0 (0.0 deals)**, against the CFO scenario's **€112,500**. The passing checks (earnings per acquired business, year-1 profit, break-even, payment terms, concentration) are mechanical: they rest on my estimates, not on any real target, and should not be read as comfort.

**Three numbers behind it** (`cfo.md`, one micro business, EUR 200k EBITDA, 3.9x, equity only, **a scenario not a forecast**):
- **Margin / return:** EUR 12,500 a month from the business, EUR 7,500 after holding costs; about **10.4%** a year on EUR 869,400 invested, payback about **9.7 years**.
- **Break-even:** one acquired business covers the holding company's EUR 5,000 a month.
- **Year 1:** EUR 52,500 operating profit **only if** the first deal closes in month 4, which is above what the panel supports; if no deal closes, about **-EUR 60,000** of holding costs plus EUR 20,000 of search.

**Board and panel.** The board voted **4 x FUND IF** (average 3.25 out of 10). The simulated sellers (20, Germany as a proxy for the EU27) gave **0% realistic close** on the first offer and **1%** on the improved offer, with a **24%** realistic meeting rate. They agree on the cause: **trust** (no track record, an offshore shell, no proof of funds). They differ on the first fix: the Offers and Account lenses put funders first, the Product and Monopoly lenses put team and focus first, and sellers asked for their own bank and adviser to vouch.

**Biggest risk.** No funder commits and no one trusts a new buyer. The plan: Test A is a written, conditional funder commitment by **2027-01-11**, then a legal opinion on the Belize/EU structure and fund regulation, an EU entity, a bank letter of funds and a bank-guaranteed deferred payment, before any letter goes out.

**What the founder needs to start.** About **EUR 806,400** before the business pays for itself, plus **EUR 78,000** deferred within 12 months (about EUR 884,400), none of it funded today. The first test is the funder gate above, then 100 letters from launch day **Monday 2027-02-08**.

**This week.** Write the funder offer (return, fees, term, minimum raise, protections) and ask three lawyers and three tax advisers for written quotes, including the Belize/EU structure opinion.

*The panel is simulated buyers and every number is a projection; real owners, real funders and real quotes confirm them. Not financial, legal or tax advice. An accountant and lawyer must check the structure, tax and the Belize/EU position before money moves.*

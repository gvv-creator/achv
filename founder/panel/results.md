## What would flip a no

- Hard proof on one of my own blocks that it cuts glazing and joinery damage claims by more than the extra cost, at or near €12 a m² all-in and with 60-day terms.
- A written guarantee from ACHV or an insurer that they pay for any damage to frames or glass from the coating or its removal, plus a price near 12 to 13 all-in and a local contractor I know who has used it.
- The main contractor or developer specifying it and paying for it in the package, together with the glass and coating manufacturers confirming in writing that it is compatible with our coated glass and finishes.
- The developer or main contractor writing it into the specification and paying for it, or proof that it cuts glazing damage claims and snagging by far more than it costs.
- A reference from a premium block in Malta where it cut glazing claims or snagging delays by more than it cost, a written warranty on glass and frame damage, and a price nearer €8-10/m².
- The main contractor putting it in the tender as a paid line, or a price near 3 euro per m2 all-in.
- Written compatibility confirmation from the glass and frame manufacturers for coated glass and finishes, plus a reference from another glazing contractor, and the option of supply-only product with no ACHV staff on my site.
- A local job I can go and see, with a contractor I know vouching for it, and a free test panel on my own frames. The price would also have to be much lower, or the client would have to pay for it.
- The glazing sub or the developer paying for it as a priced line on the contract, or a price close to film and tape cost per m² with no 150 m² minimum.
- A reference from a main contractor of similar size showing real snagging and replacement savings on glazing, and a total price close to 12 to 13 per m2 with no deposit.
- A price close to what film costs me now, plus a reference glazing contractor here who has had it applied and removed on coated glass in summer heat with no marks.
- Hard proof from a Maltese contractor that it cuts my glazing damage claims and cleaning labour by more than the extra cost, at a price nearer 6 to 8 euro per m2 all-in with no deposit.

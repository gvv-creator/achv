# Always Broke Ltd: one page

**The problem.** A growing number of EU small-business owners are near retirement with no successor; in Germany alone 545,000 SMEs want a successor by the end of 2029 and about one in four is considering closure (KfW, 2025).

**The customer.** Owner-managers aged 55+ of profitable craft, trade and niche B2B firms with 8 or more staff, plus their tax advisers and banks; and the funders who provide capital.

**The offer and price.** The Permanent Home Agreement: **3.5x to 4.5x EBITDA, 90% cash at closing, 10% within 12 months bank-guaranteed**, written 24-month job, site and name guarantee, no re-pricing after due diligence except for undisclosed material problems, a buy-back right if sold within 10 years, a paid handover of up to 12 months, and an adviser contribution capped at EUR 5,000.

**The numbers** (`cfo.md`; a scenario with one EUR 200k-EBITDA business at 3.9x, equity only):
| | |
|---|---|
| Monthly cash from the business | EUR 12,500 (EUR 7,500 after holding costs) |
| Return on invested cash | about 10.4% a year, before debt service and tax |
| Payback | about 9.7 years |
| Cash needed before it pays for itself | EUR 806,400 plus EUR 78,000 deferred (about EUR 884,400) |
| Year 1 operating profit | EUR 52,500 if the first deal closes in month 4 (above the panel); about -EUR 60,000 plus EUR 20,000 of search if none closes |

**The evidence.** Board: 4 x FUND IF, average 3.25 out of 10. Simulated sellers (20, Germany as a proxy): realistic close **1%**, realistic meeting rate **24%**, and **0.0 deals in year 1** because sellers need 12 to 15 months; the first offer scored 0%. The possible gap is the micro end of the market (under about EUR 1m EBITDA), where no permanent buyer I found says it buys; it is **not proven**. No real owner has been tested yet.

**What the money is for.** The purchase of one business, deal costs, search, an EU entity and a bank guarantee.

**The ask.** A written, conditional funder commitment sized for deal one (about EUR 0.9m) by 2027-01-11. **Verdict: Not yet.**

*Simulated buyers and projections; real owners and quotes confirm them. Not financial, legal or tax advice.*

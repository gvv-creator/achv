# Brand: Always Broke Ltd (B2B, credibility first)

Inputs: `founder/idea.md`, `founder/marketing.md` (positioning: "the permanent buyer that pays 90% in cash at closing with written job, site and name guarantees"; first audience: owner-managers aged 55+ and their advisers and banks), `founder/competitors.md`, `founder/panel-v2/results.md`.

## 1. The name problem comes first
**"Always Broke" works against the one thing the panel asked for: trust.** In v1 all 20 sellers said no for trust; in v2 17 still did. A seller, their tax adviser and their bank will read the name on a letter, a bank-letter header and a contract. A name that says "broke" invites the question the offer is trying to close ("is the money real?"). This is my judgement, not a panel finding (the panel was never shown a name, only the company description).

**Recommendation:** keep Always Broke Ltd as the legal holding if the founder likes it, but put a **different trading name on the EU acquisition company** that the v2 offer already requires (the "EU entity with named directors"). The brand work below is for that trading name. Names are not registered; see section 3.

## 3. Shortlist of three and what you must still check
1. **Standfest** (recommended, as "Standfest Holding" or "Standfest Nachfolge"): meaning ("steady") is the promise, easy to say and read, and fits a contract header.
2. **Eichhain**: the most distinctive, weakest on meaning and pronunciation.
3. **Kontinua**: clearest meaning, highest confusion risk (see above).

**Checks I ran (public, limited):** web searches for each name with "Holding", "Beteiligungen" or "Partners" found no company with exactly that name, except the near-matches above. **DNS lookups:** standfest.com, standfest.de, kontinua.com, kontinua.de, eichhain.de, eichhain.eu, stammhaus.com and stammhaus.de all resolve, so **they are taken**; standfestholding.com/.de, kontinuaholding.com, eichhain.com, eichhainholding.de and stammhausholding.* returned no record. **No DNS record is not proof the domain is free**; check a registrar.

**You must still do:**
- **Trademark:** search the EU trademark register (EUIPO eSearch plus, or TMview at `https://www.tmdn.org/tmview/`) and the German DPMA register (`https://register.dpma.de/`) for the name in **Nice class 36** (financial and investment services, holding) and **class 35** (business management, acquisition consulting). A trademark lawyer should confirm. Nothing is registered until you register it.
- **Domain:** check the exact .de, .com and .eu on a registrar; the "-holding" variants above had no DNS record.
- **Handles:** Instagram, TikTok, X, YouTube and a LinkedIn company page (the one that matters).
- **Confusion:** none of the three is close to a competitor in `competitors.md`, except Kontinua/Continua noted above.

## 4. Voice and promise
- **Promise:** "We buy to keep. Your people, your name and your site stay, and the money is real before you sign."
- **Taglines (three):** (1) "Bought to keep." (2) "Your name stays on the door." (3) "Gekauft, um zu bleiben." Not used: "A permanent home..." (close to the wording used by Aethris and similar on competitors' sites).
- **Voice, three adjectives:**
  - **Plain.** Do: short sentences, numbers, the term "cash at closing". Don't: "synergies", "unlock value", "platform".
  - **Verifiable.** Do: name the bank, adviser and directors; say what is and isn't yet true. Don't: claim a track record, references or deals we don't have.
  - **Respectful.** Do: talk about the owner's life's work and staff first. Don't: pressure, countdowns or "exclusive opportunity".
- **Before / after** (one line from `pitch.md`):
  - Before: "Always Broke Ltd buys profitable owner-managed small businesses in the EU through an EU-registered acquisition company, and keeps them for the long term."
  - After (using Standfest as a working name, **not yet registered**): "Standfest buys small family-run firms and keeps them. Your name stays on the door and your people keep their jobs."

## 5. The look, as a brief
**Colour** (contrast computed against WCAG AA, 4.5:1 for normal text):

|---|---|---|---|
| Forest | #1E3A34 | Text, headers, logo | 10.9:1 on Paper, 12.3:1 on white |
| Paper | #F6F1E7 | Background | text on it above |
| Brass | #7A5C16 | Accent, links, rules | 5.5:1 on Paper, 6.2:1 on white (AA passed; the lighter #8A6A1F only reached 4.5:1, so I darkened it) |

**Type:** display Source Serif 4, text Inter, both free under the SIL Open Font License from Google Fonts. No font the founder lacks a licence for.

**Logo brief:** it must say steady and rooted, not flashy. It must work on a letterhead, a contract header, a 32px LinkedIn icon and a bank-letter reply. Avoid: trees or oak leaves if "Eichhain" isn't chosen, a gold coin, anything that looks like a bank or a fund. I can generate concepts with an image tool if one is available; any would be marked as a concept, not a finished logo.

## 6. The first six touchpoints (B2B)
1. **LinkedIn company page:** name, one-line promise, named directors, the bank and legal adviser (once true).
2. **Website, three pages:** "How we buy" (steps, who signs, who checks), "What we guarantee" (24 months jobs and site, name kept, no re-pricing, buy-back right), "Who stands behind us" (directors, entity, bank).
3. **Letter and sales deck:** the letter is the main channel in `marketing.md`; one A4 page per owner.
4. **One-page capability statement for advisers and banks:** structure, directors, bank letter, guarantee, contact.
5. **LOI and proposal template:** plain terms, 90% cash at closing, 10% within 12 months bank-guaranteed.
6. **Invoice and contract header:** the legal entity name, registration number and the trading name side by side.

**Proof:** real directors, a real bank letter, real adviser names. **None of these exist yet**; no references, case studies, logos or results may be shown until real ones exist.

## Rules kept
No competitor's name, logo, colours or tagline copied; no unlicensed fonts or images; no invented client or result. Not legal advice on trademarks.

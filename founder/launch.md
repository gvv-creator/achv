## 1. The test, before the big costs
The cheap real test is **commitment from real accounts**, not clicks. Two tests, in order.

**Test A: the funder gate (before any big spend).** The big costs are the EU entity capital (a GmbH needs EUR 25,000 minimum share capital, per [IHK Schleswig-Holstein](https://www.ihk.de/schleswig-holstein/recht/handelsrecht/gmbh-gruendung-1359708)), the legal opinion, and adviser fees. Success line, written before running it: **at least one named funder gives a written, conditional commitment sized for deal one (about EUR 0.9m at 3.9x, per `cfo.md`) by 2027-01-11 (week 8).** If there is none, do not form the entity or send a letter; go back to `/founder-offer` and the board's funder conditions.

**Test B: the seller test (the 90-day outreach).** Success line, written before running it, in accounts:
| Measure | Success | Panel realistic (v2) | Fail line |
|---|---|---|---|
| Letters sent | 100 by day 42 of outreach | n/a | under 60 |
| Owners who take a meeting | **at least 20 of 100** | 24% (about 24 of 100) | under 10 means the message or offer is wrong |
| Owners who let their adviser call our bank and lawyer | **at least 5** | not measured | zero means the trust fix is not working |
| Signed NDAs after a first meeting | at least 5 | not measured | |
| Signed non-binding letter of intent by day 90 | 1 would beat the panel | about 0 (12 to 15 month cycles) | zero is the panel's expectation, not a failure |

If real owners fall well short of the panel, trust the real owners and return to `/founder-offer`. The panel is 20 simulated buyers; the bar here is the realistic rate, never the stated 95% meeting rate.

## 4. The first 90 days (replace the 30 days for B2B), outreach from L
Mapping `marketing.md`'s plan: adviser meetings in the first two weeks, 100 letters over weeks 3 to 6, meetings in weeks 7 to 12, with a buffer to week 13. Weekly pipeline review each Friday.

**Numbers to track weekly:** letters sent, owners answering, meetings booked and held, adviser or bank checks completed, NDAs, LOIs, cash spent (against the CFO's EUR 20,000 search estimate).

**Levels that mean change something:**
- **Meeting rate under 10% after 100 letters:** change the message or the offer (my rule of thumb from `marketing.md`).
- **No adviser will take a call:** pause and fix trust before sending more letters.
- **Spend above EUR 20,000 with no signed NDA:** stop and review.

**Review days (relative to L = 2027-02-08):**
- **Day 30 (Fri 2027-03-12):** Did 50 or more letters go out? How many meetings (target at least 10)? What did owners and advisers actually doubt? Update the letter.
- **Day 60 (Fri 2027-04-09):** Are meetings at the 24% rate? Any adviser vouching calls completed? Any NDAs? Review the price range with the first real responses and re-run `/founder-pricing` if sellers push on the multiple.
- **Day 90 (Fri 2027-05-07):** Did the success line in section 1 hold? If meetings, adviser calls and NDAs are met but no LOI, that matches the panel's 12 to 15 month cycle; plan for year 2. If meetings fall short, go back to `/founder-offer`.

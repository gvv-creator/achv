# Board brief: Always Broke Ltd

(Source: founder's company overview. No opinion added.)

## Idea file
- What it is: A newly incorporated Belize investment holding company that acquires and holds small and micro niche businesses across the EU, long term, in the Swedish investment-company style.
- Who it is for: (1) Sellers: owner-managed EU small/micro businesses facing succession or needing capital and management support. (2) Funders: providers of initial and later capital (not yet identified).
- What it sells, at what price: Not a product sale. Offers sellers a permanent home (continuity of management and culture, succession/liquidity) and funders compounding returns. Purchase multiples, fees, return targets: not yet defined.
- Where and how: Holding company in Belize; targets in the EU. First deal will be an independently sourced third-party business meeting defined financial, commercial and risk criteria. Pipeline not yet built; funding not yet secured.
- Model: B2B (M&A / investment holding). Counterparties are business owners (sellers) and capital providers (funders). Adapt the Account lens: who signs, switching cost, sales cycle, payment terms and concentration apply to sellers, funders, and the acquired businesses' own customers.
- Budget and constraints: Not stated. Startup stage.

## The founder's text (verbatim)
Always Broke Ltd is a newly established Belize-incorporated investment holding company focused on acquiring and developing profitable small and micro businesses across the European Union. The company will target specialised, niche businesses with defensible market positions, reliable cash generation and opportunities for sustainable growth.

The strategy draws inspiration from the Swedish long-term investment-company model: acquire strong businesses, preserve their entrepreneurial culture and local management, introduce disciplined financial oversight, and reinvest cash flows into organic growth and further acquisitions. Ownership will be long term, with investment decisions driven by business quality and sustainable returns rather than a predetermined resale timetable.

Priority opportunities will include established owner-managed businesses facing succession challenges or requiring capital and management support to realise their potential. Value creation will centre on operational improvements, stronger governance, management continuity and disciplined capital allocation, supported by conservative borrowing.

As a startup in the investment industry, Always Broke will begin by establishing its governance, securing initial funding and developing a qualified acquisition pipeline. Its first investment will be an independently sourced third-party business that meets clearly defined financial, commercial and risk criteria.

The long-term ambition is to build a diversified group of resilient European niche businesses, generating recurring cash flows and compounding shareholder value while progressively establishing a credible institutional investment track record.
